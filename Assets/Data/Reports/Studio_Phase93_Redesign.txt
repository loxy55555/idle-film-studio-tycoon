ROOT CAUSES FIXED
-----------------
Problem: Old StudioSubTabBar + dept panels + BonificationsBar were all rendering
simultaneously alongside the new dashboard tiles. Multiple nav layers stacked visually.

Fix: StudioDashboard.EnsureBuilt() now iterates ALL direct children of EstudioPanel
and forcibly hides everything EXCEPT StudioHeader and StudioVisualStage. This is
bulletproof regardless of scene hierarchy naming.
